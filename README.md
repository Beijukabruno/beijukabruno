# Hi, I’m Beijuka Bruno 👋

I’m an AI researcher and builder based in Kampala, Uganda, focused on developing practical, locally relevant AI solutions for healthcare. My work sits at the intersection of **machine learning, clinical research, AI4 Social GoodAI in Health and real-world deployment**, with a strong emphasis on underserved populations and female health.

I care about building systems that don’t just perform well in papers—but actually *work where they are needed most*.

---

## What I Work On

My work spans both **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** and **Computer Vision (CV)**, applied to healthcare challenges in low-resource settings.

Over the past few years, I have worked on:

- **Speech & Language AI**
  - Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) for African languages  
  - Building datasets and evaluation pipelines for low-resource speech systems  

- **Computer Vision for Healthcare**
  - Cervical cancer risk prediction from clinical data and or medical images  
  - TB prediction using hybrid models (clinical + imaging data)  
  - Ultrasound and maternal health imaging research  

- **Clinical & Predictive Modeling**
  - TB drug resistance risk and treatment outcome prediction models  
  - Maternal health risk modelling  
  - Mental health-related AI systems  

- **Document AI & Local Language Processing**
  - OCR systems for Luganda  
  - Data extraction pipelines tailored for local contexts  

- **Data & Infrastructure**
  - Building and curating datasets for African healthcare use cases  
  - Designing reproducible ML pipelines and evaluation systems  
  - Deploying models using Docker and production-ready workflows  

---

## Research Direction

My current focus is on:

- **AI for Female Health**
  - Cervical cancer  
  - Maternal health
  - Mental Health 
  - Early risk detection systems  

- **Explainable & Trustworthy AI (XAI)**
  - Making models interpretable for clinicians  
  - Bridging the gap between ML outputs and clinical decision-making  

- **Localized AI Systems**
  - Adapting models to African datasets and realities  
  - Moving beyond “imported AI” to **context-aware solutions**
